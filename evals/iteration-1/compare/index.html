Comparison limits and capture method

One run per configuration. The menu-bar and banking baselines found and used the Recall design system: those comparisons are against a written design system. The dashboard is the clean no-skill baseline. See the iteration findings.

All captures are offline Chromium, 1× device pixels, en-GB, Europe/London, reduced motion and fresh storage. The clock starts at a fixed time and advances 1.5 seconds to settle state timers. Product code and fonts are preserved. Each dashboard has its own fixture date, invented incidents and metrics. Menu-bar runs also differ in shifts, people and ranges; banking runs differ in names, balances and identification routes.

TV: 1920 × 1080. Laptop: 1440 × 900. Phone: 390 × 844 and 320 × 568. These are viewport captures at scroll position zero: below-fold content is intentionally absent. Menu-bar captures crop only the native popover (340 px with skill, 360 px without); internal scroll areas remain at their initial position. Prototype-only footer controls are hidden in the banking baseline. The original dashboard appears in light only and is shared context.
