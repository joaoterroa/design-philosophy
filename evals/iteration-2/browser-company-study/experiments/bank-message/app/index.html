Set up the Harbour app

You can check your balance, see your payments and pay people you know, from your phone. Setting it up takes about 10 minutes.

You'll need

  • Your Harbour bank card. The one you use at the cash machine.
  • This phone, with the mobile number we have for you. We'll send it a text.

You can stop at any point. We'll keep what you've entered on this phone for 7 days.

Would you rather do this with someone from Harbour? Call us free on 0808 157 0192, or ask at your branch. We'll go through it with you.

    Choose a 6-number passcode

    You'll type this each time you open the app. Pick numbers you'll remember but other people couldn't guess.

    Avoid your birthday, your card PIN, or numbers in a row like 123456.

    Log in with your fingerprint or face?

    Instead of typing your passcode, you can touch the sensor or look at your phone.

    Your fingerprint and face stay on this phone. Harbour never sees them.

    Anyone whose fingerprint or face is saved on this phone could also open the app. If someone else's is saved, choose the passcode.

    You can change this later in Settings.
